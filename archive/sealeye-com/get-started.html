Get Started

Start with a read of your platform library.

There are two ways in. Platform and technology leaders start with an engagement. Developers whose company already runs seaLeye start with one command.

For platform and technology leaders For developers

For platform and technology leaders

A three-step engagement

Each step stands on its own. You decide whether to take the next one with the results of the last in front of you.

  1. Read the library

    Next week
    • Read-only access to the repositories that hold your modules and pipelines, or run seaLeye on premises in containers.
    • One senior engineer from your team.
    • The discovery agent ingests the platform and lights up every module for the front door agent.
    • You keep the report whether or not you go further.

  2. Pilot with one team

    A paid proof of concept, eight weeks
    • The team runs sealeye run on its own apps.
    • Your platform engineers review every plan for the first two weeks.
    • Success criteria are written up front, in your numbers: minutes, not weeks, and no bad plans.
    • A checkpoint at week four, and the readout at week eight.

  3. Scale out

    After the readout
    • Roll out to the rest of the business line.
    • Turn your platform engineers back to the roadmap.

What each step costs is on the Pricing page.

To begin, call or write to reception below. Ask for the trust package at the same time, and your onboarding reviewers can start in parallel.

For developers

Your company already runs seaLeye

Install it from your platform team's registry, then run it in your app's folder. seaLeye reads the app, asks a few questions, shows you the plan, and waits for your yes.

Read the docs Open the console

$ npm install -g @your-org/sealeye

$ cd your-app && sealeye run

Your platform team publishes seaLeye under your organization's own name, so the package name above is theirs to choose.
