Changelog

What has changed in sealeye, newest first.

2.14.2 September 24, 2026

  • The spinner no longer sits on the name of the last file it read. Once the reading has stopped it says what is actually happening, which is that seaLeye is working out what your app needs.

2.14.1 September 22, 2026

  • sealeye init is now sealeye run. init still works.

2.14.0 September 17, 2026

  • Deploying hands the terminal back. After a few seconds you get your prompt, the deploy finishes in the background, and a notification tells you when your environment is live.
  • --watch on run or deploy keeps you on the live panel instead, for a rehearsal or a recording.

2.13.0 September 10, 2026

  • sealeye run --team-context tells you whether another team has already hit the same boundary, and offers you the ticket that is open with your platform team.

2.12.0 September 1, 2026

  • The settings your app reads from its environment are passed through to it when it runs. Values come from your own environment file when you have one, and from the plan when you do not.
  • Integrations in the console, grouped by the job you are trying to do rather than by vendor.

2.11.0 August 21, 2026

  • A plan that names something your catalog does not have is declined, in a sentence that says what is missing and what would make it deployable. The plan is still valid, still saved, and still yours.

2.10.0 August 11, 2026

  • The Platform Library. Every module now says whether seaLeye builds it today, whether your platform team has agreed to support it, or whether somebody has asked for it and nobody has agreed yet. The interview may choose any of them, so your plan records what your app actually needs.
  • Catalog and Team pages in the console.

2.9.0 July 29, 2026

  • Profiles. One question decides what your plan is designed for: something you are trying out, a team using it every day, or production. Your later environments are built from that plan, so the answer outlives this run.

2.8.0 July 16, 2026

  • sealeye run says so when your app already has a live environment, and shows you its URL, before it asks you anything.
  • On a second run the card becomes a diff: what is unchanged, what changed, what is new, what is gone. When nothing moved, it says so.
  • Performance and Control pages in the console, and a history of the environments you have torn down.

2.7.0 July 2, 2026

  • sealeye run --answers <file> answers the interview from a file, one line per question, for rehearsals. It never answers "Deploy this?". That one stays yours.

2.6.0 June 22, 2026

  • Something moves during every wait. Every pause the CLI used to sit silently through now says what it is doing and how long it has been doing it.
  • watch redraws in place instead of clearing the screen, so the run you just had is still above it.
  • A Configure page in the console.

2.5.0 June 9, 2026

  • Your app starts from the file your plan names, not always index.js.
  • A plan naming a file your folder does not have is refused before anything is built. A working environment serving errors is worse than a sentence.

2.4.0 May 28, 2026

  • sealeye destroy takes the name of the app, not only a deployment id.
  • A static environment has its storage emptied before the environment is deleted, so tear down no longer stops half way through.
  • Logs in the console, next to the environment they came from.

2.3.0 May 15, 2026

  • sealeye list shows one row per environment instead of one per deploy. --deployments gives you every deploy, and --all includes the environments you have torn down.

2.2.0 May 4, 2026

  • The plan card. Before you say yes you see what seaLeye thinks your app is, what it will build, and what you get back, rendered from the plan itself rather than from anything written about it.
  • Costs in the console, by day and by resource.

2.1.0 April 21, 2026

  • The interview. sealeye run reads the folder you are standing in, asks at most four questions in your own words, and writes the plan next to your code. Nothing reaches your cloud account before you say yes.

2.0.0 April 7, 2026

  • seaLeye.online. Every environment seaLeye has built for your organization in one place, with what it is doing, what it costs and who owns it.

1.0.0 January 14, 2026

  • The CLI. A plan goes in and a real environment comes out, in your own cloud account, with a URL you can open.
  • deploy, status, watch, list and destroy, and a report written next to your code saying what was built and how to take it down.
