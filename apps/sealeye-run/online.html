seaLeye.online

seaLeye.run gets your app running. seaLeye.online is the rest of that environment's life. Every environment seaLeye.run has built for your organization is here: what it is doing, what it costs, what could be smaller, what could be put to sleep, what should be decommissioned, and who owns it.

What you will find

  • Every environment, in one list with its status and the app it belongs to, however many teams built them.
  • What it costs by day and by resource, against the budget you set for it.
  • Pause or decommission an environment you are not using, with a record of what was done to it and when.
  • Who owns it and who else has access, with what each level of access can do.

Logs and performance for each app are here too, next to the environment they came from.
