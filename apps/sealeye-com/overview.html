Product overview

Your developers' front door to your enterprise platform.

seaLeye gives every development team self-service over its own cloud infrastructure, across the whole software lifecycle, through the platform automation you already run and the controls you already trust.

Your platform is good. Getting through the door is slow.

Your platform team spent years building hardened modules, golden pipelines and a control model your auditors sign off on. Then every new app, every change and every promotion became a request in their queue.

Developers wait weeks for a first environment. Platform engineers spend their days provisioning for other teams, or get forward-deployed into those teams to do it. And the strategic work waits: the second cloud, the migrations, the new capabilities.

A front door agent that already knows your platform.

seaLeye puts an agent at the front door of your platform. Before a developer ever talks to it, the discovery agent has ingested your Platform Library (modules, pipelines and infrastructure as code) and learned the control model that governs it. The front door agent is trained on all of that. It works with each developer inside the app they are building, in their own language, and plans the infrastructure the app needs from what your platform team has already approved.

The discovery agent

Reads your modules, pipelines and infrastructure as code, read-only, and learns the controls that govern them.

The front door agent

Works with each developer in their app, asks only what the code cannot answer, and plans from what is approved.

  1. Ingest

    The discovery agent reads your Platform Library, read-only, overnight.

  2. Plan

    The front door agent reads the developer's app, asks a few questions in their language, and writes a plan they can read.

  3. Run

    On approval, your own pipelines and infrastructure as code do the work, and every step is recorded.

Where seaLeye sits

seaLeye is a layer in front of the platform you already run. It drives your pipelines and your infrastructure as code, and it never replaces them.

Self-service for the whole life of an environment.

Provision

A working dev environment for a new app in minutes, not weeks.

Change

When the app changes, its infrastructure changes with it, through the same modules and the same approvals.

Manage

See what every environment costs, what it is doing and who owns it. Right-size it, put it to sleep, or decommission it.

Promote

Move a build through your test stages to production, through the gates your pipelines already enforce.

The command line or the browser. One product.

seaLeye.run is the command line: cd your-app && sealeye run. seaLeye.online is the web console. Both do exactly the same things; only the interface differs.

Some developers live in the terminal and never leave it. Some prefer the browser and stay there. Plenty switch between the two as they work. Either way, they stop knocking on the platform team's door.

Every control you built stays in force.

seaLeye doesn't replace your platform, and it never goes around it. It builds only from your approved modules, through your pipelines, under your policies, so what you certified stays certified.

When an app needs something your library doesn't offer, seaLeye doesn't improvise. It drafts the plan, records the need along with the code that shows it, and puts it in front of your platform team.

  • Your modules, pipelines and policies stay unchanged.
  • A person approves every plan before anything runs.
  • Environments land in your own accounts.
  • Evidence for every change is generated from the record.

Give your platform engineers their roadmap back.
