How it works

An agent at the front door that already knows your platform.

Before a developer ever talks to seaLeye, the discovery agent has read your Platform Library. The front door agent plans from what your platform team has already approved, and your own automation does the work.

  1. Ingest

    The discovery agent reads your Platform Library, read-only, overnight.

  2. Plan

    The front door agent reads the developer's app, asks a few questions in their language, and writes a plan they can read.

  3. Run

    On approval, your own pipelines and infrastructure as code do the work, and every step is recorded.

FAQ

Questions we hear first

Does seaLeye replace our platform team?

No. It gives them their roadmap back. Your platform team still owns the modules, the pipelines and the controls. seaLeye takes the everyday requests off their queue, so their time goes to the work only they can do.

Does it replace our pipelines or our infrastructure as code?

No. It drives them. seaLeye builds only from your approved modules and runs through the pipelines you already have. What you certified stays certified.

What happens when a developer needs something our platform doesn't support?

seaLeye records the need, with the code that shows it, and routes it to the platform team. The plan is saved as a draft. It never builds around the library. When your platform supports the need, the developer runs seaLeye again and the plan is finished.

Command line or browser?

Both, with exactly the same capabilities. seaLeye.run is the command line and seaLeye.online is the web console. Developers use whichever suits them, and plenty switch between the two.

Where does seaLeye run?

As SaaS in a Canadian or US region, as a dedicated single-tenant instance, in your own cloud account, or as containers on premises. Deployment options

Is our code used to train models?

No. Your code, your configuration and your Platform Library are never used to train models.
